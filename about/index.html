About

What is this?

A random character generator for Baldur's Gate 3, up to date with Patch 8.

Press Generate to roll a full level 12 character: your class and subclass, race, background, ability scores, feats and starting spells. It also picks whether you accept the tadpole, who you romance and which companion comes along. Use it for a random run, a challenge run, or when you can't decide what to play next.

How does it pick things?

Every roll is random, but it follows the game's rules, so you can always make the build in BG3.

  • You play a custom character unless you turn on origin characters in Settings, which lets you play as an origin character or the Dark Urge.
  • Sometimes you'll get a multiclass build. Subclasses and class choices only appear at the level you'd unlock them.
  • Ability scores are arranged for your class.
  • You get a feat at every level the game gives one. Feats you couldn't use, or that clash with each other, are left out.
  • Your race, background and class won't give you the same skill twice.

Like part of a build? Lock it and reroll the rest. You can also leave out classes or races, or roll a whole party, in Settings.

Keyboard shortcuts

On the Generator page:

  • G Generate a new character
  • Z Undo
  • S Save the build
  • C Copy a link to the build

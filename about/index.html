About

What is this?

A random character generator for Baldur's Gate 3, kept up to date with Patch 8.

Hit Generate and you get a full level 12 character: who you're playing, your class, race, background, ability scores and feats, plus whether to take the tadpole, who to romance and a companion to bring along. It's great for random runs, challenge runs, or when you just can't decide what to play next.

How does it pick things?

Everything follows the game's rules, so every build is one you can actually make:

  • You play a custom character by default. Turn on origin characters in Settings and you might play as the Dark Urge, Karlach, Astarion or another origin character instead. They keep their own race and background.
  • Every now and then you'll get a multiclass build. Subclasses and class choices only show up once you'd actually have them.
  • Ability scores are spread the way you'd want them for your class.
  • You get a feat at every level the game gives you one, and you won't get the same skill twice.

Like part of a build? Lock it and reroll the rest. You can also leave out classes or races, or roll a whole party, in Settings.

Found something wrong?

If something doesn't match the game, or you have an idea for something to add, open an issue on GitHub.

About

What this is

A random character generator for Baldur's Gate 3. One click rolls a full level 12 build: who you play, class and subclass, race, background, ability scores, feats, the tadpole question, a romance and a companion to bring along. It's made for random runs, challenge runs, or getting unstuck when you can't decide what to play next.

It's up to date with Patch 8, the latest major patch.

How it rolls

  • Origin: usually a custom character, sometimes the Dark Urge or one of the six origin characters. Origin characters keep their own race and background, and the Dark Urge is always The Haunted One.
  • Class: level 12 in one class, or occasionally a multiclass split where the starting class keeps at least half the levels. Subclasses and class choices only appear at the level BG3 grants them.
  • Ability scores: a 27-point buy arranged for the starting class, with the racial +2 and +1 on its two most important abilities.
  • Feats: one at every feat level the build reaches. Armor feats only show up when you have the armor they build on.
  • Skills: race, background and class never hand out the same skill twice.
  • Companions: your romance's build, a hireling if you romance no one, or a whole party of three if you turn that on in Settings.

Lock any card to keep it, reroll just the parts you don't like, and use Settings to leave out classes or races or change how often multiclass builds happen.

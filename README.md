# Baldur's Gate 3 Character Generator

A small, browser-based character generator for Baldur's Gate 3. Generate a character, reroll individual sections, and save builds in your browser.

**Try it: https://thefallz.github.io/BG3CharacterGenerator/**

![A generated level 12 character with Class, Race, Background, Feats, Romance and Companion Build cards](docs/screenshot.png)

Up to date with Patch 8, the latest major patch. If you spot a rule that doesn't match the game, please [open an issue](https://github.com/TheFallZ/BG3CharacterGenerator/issues).

This is an unofficial fan project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Larian Studios.

## Features

- **A full level 12 build in one click:** class and subclass, race and subrace, background, skills, and a feat for every feat level.
- **Who you play:** a custom character, or, with origin characters turned on in Settings, the Dark Urge or one of the six origin characters, who keep their own race and background.
- **Ability scores** from a 27-point buy arranged for your class, with the racial +2 and +1 in the right places.
- **Preferred weapons** for your character that fit the class and fighting style.
- **Class choices filled in:** fighting styles, maneuvers, metamagic, invocations, Wildheart aspects, Paladin oath prompts, and starting cantrips and spells for spellcasters.
- **Multiclass builds:** an 8% chance to roll one, with the level split shown on the Class card. Triple classing can be turned on in Settings for an extra-rare three-class build.
- **Story prompts:** whether to accept the tadpole, who to romance, and a deity for Clerics.
- **A build for a companion or hireling,** either in their usual class or a random new one, with preferred weapons. Turn on **full party** in Settings to roll three companions instead.
- **Reroll any single card** without touching the rest of your build, and **Undo** if you roll past one you liked.
- **Lock cards you like** so they stay put when you generate again.
- **Save your favorite builds** and find them on the Saved page to search, sort, open, rename, add a note to, share or delete later. Back them up to a file and import them in another browser or on another device.
- **Share a build** with a link that opens exactly what you rolled, or copy it as text ready to paste into Reddit or Discord.
- **A Settings page** to leave out classes or races, choose how often multiclass builds happen, and hide the tadpole, romance or companion cards.
- **Keyboard shortcuts:** G to generate, Z to undo, S to save and C to copy a link.
- **Works in any browser,** on desktop or phone, with no account or sign-up needed.

## Saved Builds

Saved builds stay in the browser you saved them in. They aren't uploaded anywhere, so they won't show up on another device or browser, and clearing your browser data removes them.

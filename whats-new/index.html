What's new

  1. v2.0

    The big update

    Saved builds and settings get their own pages, the generator rolls more of your character, and sharing a build is a lot easier.

    Your character

    • Ability scores arranged for your class, with your bonus points added
    • Preferred melee and ranged weapons
    • Origin characters, including the Dark Urge (turn on in Settings)
    • A full party of three companions, if you'd like
    • Rare triple-class builds (turn on in Settings)
    • Feats that make sense together, with no clashing picks like Great Weapon Master and Shield Master
    • Ability Improvements show up more often and say which stats they raise

    Settings

    • Pick how often you get a multiclass build
    • Leave out classes or races you don't want
    • Hide cards you don't need

    Saving and sharing

    • Name your builds and add notes
    • Search and sort the Saved page
    • Share links and copy-as-text for Reddit and Discord
    • Back up and import builds between devices
    • Undo and keyboard shortcuts (G, Z, S, C)

    Smaller changes

    • A cleaner layout with calmer colors
    • Companion builds say companion or hireling
    • Works properly in the Reddit app
    • Rangers no longer get the same skill twice
    • Lolth-sworn Drow clerics always follow Lolth
    • More class choices from the game, like Expertise, Magical Secrets, Arcane Shots and Elemental Disciplines
    • Multiclass builds no longer get the same cantrip twice
    • Better keyboard navigation and bigger buttons on phones
  2. v1.0

    First release

    The first version of the generator, made to pick a random character for your next Baldur's Gate 3 run.

    Your character

    • A full level 12 build in one click
    • Class and subclass, with class choices like fighting styles, maneuvers, metamagic and invocations
    • Race and subrace, background, and starting cantrips and spells
    • A feat for every feat level, with no repeats
    • Occasional multiclass builds

    Your story

    • Whether to accept the tadpole
    • Who to romance
    • A build for a companion or hireling to bring along
    • A deity for Clerics that fits their race

    Rolling

    • Reroll any single card without changing the rest
    • Lock the cards you like so they stay put
    • Skills never repeat between race, background and class

    Saving

    • Save builds in your browser to come back to later
    • Load, rename and delete saved builds

What's new

  1. October 2026

    The big update

    v2.0

    Saved builds and settings get their own pages, the generator rolls more of your character, and sharing a build is a lot easier.

    Your character

    • Ability scores arranged for your class, with your bonus points added
    • Preferred melee and ranged weapons
    • Origin characters, including the Dark Urge (turn on in Settings)
    • A full party of three companions, if you'd like
    • Rare triple-class builds (turn on in Settings)

    Settings

    • Pick how often you get a multiclass build
    • Leave out classes or races you don't want
    • Hide cards you don't need

    Saving and sharing

    • Name your builds and add notes
    • Search and sort the Saved page
    • Share links and copy-as-text for Reddit and Discord
    • Back up and import builds between devices
    • Undo and keyboard shortcuts (G, Z, S, C)

    Smaller changes

    • A cleaner layout with calmer colors
    • Companion builds say companion or hireling
    • Works properly in the Reddit app
  2. October 2026

    First release

    v1.0

    Roll a full level 12 character, lock or reroll any part of it, and save the builds you like.

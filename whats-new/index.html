What's new

The big update

October 2026

This is a big one. Saved builds and settings have their own pages now, the generator rolls more of your character, and sharing a build is a lot easier.

Your character

  • Ability scores, arranged for your class with your bonus points already added.
  • Preferred melee and ranged weapons that suit your class and fighting style.
  • Origin characters. Turn them on in Settings and you might play as the Dark Urge, Karlach, Astarion or one of the others.
  • A full party of three companions instead of one, if you'd like.
  • Rare triple-class builds, when you allow them.

Settings

  • Pick how often you get a multiclass build.
  • Leave out any classes or races you don't want.
  • Hide cards you don't need, like the tadpole question.

Saving and sharing

  • Name your builds and add notes, like where you're up to in a run.
  • Search and sort everything on the Saved page.
  • Share a link that opens exactly what you rolled, or copy it as text for Reddit or Discord.
  • Back up your saved builds and import them on another device.
  • Undo if you roll past a build you liked.
  • Keyboard shortcuts: G to generate, Z to undo, S to save and C to copy a link.

Smaller changes

  • A cleaner layout with calmer colors.
  • Companion builds say whether you're bringing a companion or a hireling.
  • Works properly in the Reddit app and other in-app browsers.

First release

October 2026

The first version. Roll a full level 12 character with a class, race, background, feats, the tadpole question, a romance and a companion. Lock or reroll any part, and save the builds you like.

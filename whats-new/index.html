What's new

The big update

October 2026

New pages

  • Saved lists every build you've saved, with search, sorting and a menu to copy, rename, add a note or delete.
  • Settings lets you choose what can be rolled.
  • About explains how the generator picks things.
  • What's new, the page you're reading.

More to every build

  • Ability scores spread the way your class wants them, with your bonus points included.
  • Preferred weapons for your character that fit your class and fighting style.
  • Origin characters: turn them on in Settings to sometimes play as the Dark Urge, Karlach, Astarion and the rest.
  • Full parties: roll three companions instead of one, shown side by side.
  • Triple classing: a rare three-class build when you allow it.
  • Companion builds now say whether you're bringing a Companion or a Hireling.

Settings

  • Choose how often multiclass builds happen: Never, Sometimes or Always.
  • Leave out any classes or races you don't want to roll.
  • Hide the cards you don't care about, like the tadpole question or romance.

Saving and sharing

  • Share a build with a link that opens exactly what you rolled, or copy it as text for Reddit or Discord.
  • Name your builds. Named builds show their name as a heading and in their links.
  • Notes on saved builds, for things like where you are in a run.
  • Back up your saved builds to a file and import them in another browser or on another device.
  • Undo brings back the build you just rolled past.
  • Keyboard shortcuts: G to generate, Z to undo, S to save and C to copy a link.

Look and feel

  • A cleaner layout, with your build's name or race and class at the top and every button in one place.
  • Saved builds look like the Generator's cards.
  • Calmer colors, with less pink.
  • Works better in in-app browsers like the Reddit app, where pop-ups used to be blocked.

First release

October 2026

  • Roll a full level 12 build: class and subclass, race, background, feats, the tadpole question, a romance and a companion.
  • Reroll or lock any part of a build.
  • Save builds in your browser.
